The application adds interactive HTML/SVG markers and does not claim that the
base artwork indicates representative availability.

## Iraq governorate geometry

- Source: SimpleMaps Iraq administrative SVG (`https://simplemaps.com/resources/svg-iq`)
- The source SVG supplies the 18 established governorate boundaries used by the
  interactive map. Halabja remains available through the accessible region
  selector because the source geometry does not yet separate its newer border.
- Source attribution and license notice are retained in
  `public/images/iraq-regions-simple.svg`.

- Iran province path geometry is adapted from `masoudnemati/iran-map`,
